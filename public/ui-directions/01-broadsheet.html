Optimistic rollups keep track of the entire history of state roots and the hash of each batch. If anyone discovers that one batch had an incorrect post-state root, they can publish a fraud proof to chain, and the contract reverts that batch and all batches after it.1 ZK rollups, by contrast, include a cryptographic proof — a ZK-SNARK — with every batch, proving the post-state root is the correct result of executing the batch, verified quickly on-chain no matter the size of the computation.1
